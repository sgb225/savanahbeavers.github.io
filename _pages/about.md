I am a physicist interested in the intersection of particle physics, cosmology, and computational methods. My undergraduate education has provided me with a foundation in theoretical astrophysics, with research expereince in exoplanet detection, stellar property identification, and simulating galaxy dynamics. 

Alongside my academic work, I have developed expertise in data science and machine learning, with a primary focus on their applications in physics. Personal projects I have conducted include ML pipelines for dark matter detection using CERN collider data, GNNs for predicting dark matter halo masses from galaxy properties, and employing Bayesian inference and MCMC methods to model exoplanetary systems and astrophysical datasets. Through these efforts, I have gained experience with the Python-based scientific computing libraries of NumPy, SciPy, pandas, Matplotlib, Plotly, scikit-learn, PyTorch, Keras, juliet, and Lightkurve.

My long term goal is to pursue a PhD in physics, with research centered on expanding our understanding of dark matter and dark energy.
